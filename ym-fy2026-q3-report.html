Section 06

Finance Audits

13 NNs and SRs. One quarter. A lot learned.

A finance audit is a 30-minute call with the National Finance team — we walk through your QuickBooks dashboard together, fix what needs fixing, and answer questions in real time. This quarter we did 13 of them.

The big picture: Most NNs were genuinely trying to manage money responsibly — they just didn’t always have the access, training, or processes to keep their dashboards current. The audits helped most when we worked through real situations together on the call.

For NNs:

  • Share the knowledge. Every shura member should have dashboard access and know how to submit a JotForm. Practice together — and pass it on when leadership changes.
  • Record everything. Income, expenses, small purchases, masjid-funded items — log it all. Save receipts with the event name and date.
  • Check what you actually have. Count your cash and compare it to the dashboard regularly. Ask Finance about any differences.
  • Don’t leave a teammate out of pocket. Submit expense requests promptly, follow up if a reimbursement hasn’t come through, and ask about an advance if paying upfront would be hard.
  • Plan shared events before they happen. Agree who records what before the event — splitting income and expenses after the fact is where most of the confusion we saw came from.
  • Ask early and often. After your audit we create a WhatsApp thread with your NN and National Finance — use it. You don’t have to figure it out alone.
  • Rely less on cash. Store funds in the YM bank account and request electronic reimbursements whenever possible.

For SRs & National Finance:

  • Make onboarding hands-on. Every shura member should practice — not just watch. Cover dashboard access, who holds the funds, how to submit income and expenses, and where to ask for help.
  • Build in regular SR check-ins. A lightweight monthly check catches missing forms, balance questions, and reimbursement gaps before they need a national audit.
  • Give National and SR check-ins different jobs. SR finance leads handle ongoing local support; National Finance focuses on onboarding new NNs and auditing where there are harder issues. This keeps both from being stretched thin.
  • Make the handbook easy to find. NNs who read the NN Finance Handbook before their audit had noticeably fewer issues. Encourage every NNIT to read it at onboarding — and revisit it when questions come up.
Want an audit for your NN? Email finance@ymsisters.com with your NN name and we’ll get you on the schedule.
