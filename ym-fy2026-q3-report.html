What financial aid unlocks

Financial aid helps make meaningful opportunities more accessible to more sisters — whether that's YMLC, YMC, Muslim Advocacy Day, Arabic and Islamic studies intensives, or beyond. These experiences can shape how a sister grows in her deen, steps into leadership roles, and serves her community, and we want more sisters to have the chance to be part of them inshAllah.

Friendly reminders:

Mixed receipts will be denied — we are strict on this. Make YM purchases on their own receipt.
Format: Date · Event Name · Subcategory (e.g., 1/9/26 NJ Gala – Decorations). Some NNs are omitting event names and being contacted individually — save everyone the back-and-forth by labeling correctly the first time.
When you buy something online, download or print the receipt as a PDF instead of taking a screenshot. It keeps every detail clear and makes your expense much easier for us to review.
We can't approve an expense without a receipt, so please attach one every time you submit. It's the simplest way to keep things moving — no receipt, no approval.

Quick check — how well do you know the policies?

We can't see your scores at all so don't worry about getting it right the first time. This is just for you to practice!
