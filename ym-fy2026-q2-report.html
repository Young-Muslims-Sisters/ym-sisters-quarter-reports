Section 01

Letter from the Finance Team

We begin in the name of Allah ﷻ and send peace and blessings upon His Messenger ﷺ

What a quarter it has been. Between YMC bringing our community together in Baltimore, chapters across the country serving their communities through YM Gives and other amazing events, our sisters showing up in D.C. for Muslim Advocacy Day, and the exciting groundwork laid ahead for YMLC this summer - Q2 was full of khair. And inshAllah, we hope this report is the story of that khair, told in numbers.

This quarter also took us on the road. National Shura tours to Georgia and Dallas let national leadership sit face-to-face with local leadership, hearing what's working, what's been confusing, and what chapters actually need from us. Those conversations directly shaped the systems and reminders you'll find in these pages.

So why do we care this much about receipts and spreadsheets and all of this seemingly boring, mundane stuff? We so often hear the story of the man who asked the Prophet ﷺ whether he should tie his camel or trust in Allah ﷻ, and the Prophet ﷺ told him: tie it and trust in Allah ﷻ. That's exactly what this work is. We make du'a for barakah in YM's efforts, and then we tie our camel: the JotForms, the dashboards, the receipts. All the admin work behind the tarbiyyah and spiritual development happening in our organization - that's us tying our camel.

And we take it this seriously because Allah ﷻ asks us to. He tells us in Surah An-Nisa:

إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَأْمُرُكُمْ أَنْ تُؤَدُّوا الْأَمَانَاتِ إلَٰى أَهْلِهَا
“Indeed, Allah commands you to render trusts to whom they are due.”
Qur'an 4:58

We must remember that every dollar YM holds is exactly that: a trust from the community. So when you submit a JotForm to track your finances, upload a proper receipt, make sure your numbers add up, or keep your dashboard current, that's not just paperwork, but an amanah that we have to uphold. And because of your cooperation and attention to detail, we can account for every dollar YM earned or spent this quarter, and hand the next group of leaders records they can actually plan from.

We ask Allah ﷻ to accept our efforts, place barakah in every dollar entrusted to this organization, and keep our NeighborNets growing in strength, sisterhood, and service.

With much love,YM Sisters National Finance Department

Financial aid helps make meaningful opportunities more accessible to more sisters - whether that’s YMLC, YMC, Muslim Advocacy Day, Arabic and Islamic studies intensives, or beyond. NN Spotlight - Portland, Maine

Portland NeighborNet wins a $45,000 grant

YM Portland in Maine applied for outside grant funding and, Alhamdulillah, was awarded $45,000! The grant will be paid out at $15,000 per year over three years, giving the chapter dedicated funding to support and grow its programming.

$45,000Total Grant Awarded
$15,000Paid Out Yearly
3Years of Funding
Your NN can do this too! Grant money is actually available across the country in various states. State programs, community foundations, and local youth funds all give to groups like YM. YM Portland applied under YM National's 501(c)(3), which is something every NeighborNet can do. If your NN wants to apply for a grant, email finance@ymsisters.com and we'll help you find opportunities and begin the paperwork process inshAllah. Even if you think your NN doesn't need the money, it can always be of benefit to the organization!

Notes from Our Team

Updates & friendly reminders

Updates (as of 2026 Q2 end):

The NN Finance Handbook is here! 🎉 We're excited to announce the release of the NN Finance Handbook - your complete guide to accounts, JotForm submission types, dashboards, approvals, lump sums, audits, and a full worked case study. Read it here →
Prefer Zelle over Venmo when possible. Coordinate Zelle details with finance@ymsisters.com after submitting the JotForm.
We now have an official process for handling finances when two NNs collaborate on an event and split costs. See Handbook 06b for the full worked example.
We don't want multiple events' expenses combined into one JotForm. If you paid for or attended more than one event, submit a separate JotForm for each event so we can track and approve them individually.

Friendly reminders:

Mixed receipts will be denied - we are strict on this. Make YM purchases on their own receipt.
Format: Date · Event Name · Subcategory (e.g., 1/9/26 NJ Gala – Decorations). Some NNs are omitting event names and being contacted individually - save everyone the back-and-forth by labeling correctly the first time.
When you buy something online, download or print the receipt as a PDF instead of taking a screenshot. It keeps every detail clear and makes your expense much easier for us to review.
We can't approve an expense without a receipt, so please attach one every time you submit. It's the simplest way to keep things moving - no receipt, no approval.

Quick check - how well do you know the policies?

We can't see your scores at all so don't worry about getting it right the first time. This is just for you to practice!
